# Entrepreneurship

Building your own income streams and location-independent work.

## What belongs here

- Freelancing paths (writing, design, development, video editing)
- Content creation (YouTube, podcasting, newsletters)
- Remote work skills and job hunting
- E-commerce and dropshipping
- Van life and mobile businesses
- Teaching skills online
- Photography and videography services
- Gig economy strategies

## Priority for young adults

Medium. These paths offer maximum flexibility but typically require either existing skills or runway to build an audience. Good as secondary income or long-term goal while doing work-and-travel.
