# Local Opportunities — Walworth, WI

Employers within roughly a 60-minute drive of Walworth, Wisconsin that are actively recruiting and train on the job. Live at home, commute, get paid from the first day of training.

**Start here:** [Category Overview](OVERVIEW.md) — geography, the four training mechanisms, sector landscape, and local scam warnings.

**Then read this:** [WIOA On-the-Job Training](wioa-on-the-job-training.md) — the mechanism that gets you hired into most of the rest. Its Illinois counterpart is [Illinois Workforce Boards](illinois-workforce-boards.md).

**Age assumption:** documents in this category are written for a 21-year-old. That clears the CDL minimum, the alcohol-service minimum, and every age floor documented here. Wisconsin Youth Apprenticeship (high school juniors and seniors only) does not apply.

## Three Phone Calls Worth Making

Most of these opportunities are gated by a person, not a portal.

1. **(262) 741-5180** — Walworth County Job Center. Ask about current OJT contracts.
2. **(414) 216-8417** — Wisconsin DWD Bureau of Apprenticeship Standards. Their job includes helping applicants find employers who will sponsor an apprenticeship. Almost nobody calls.
3. **(262) 564-2954** — Gateway Technical College apprenticeship department. Ask which local employers sponsor which programs.

Two more, depending on direction: **(815) 398-9491** (OrthoIllinois HR, for the cast tech role) and **(815) 985-8582** (Rock River Valley Tooling and Machining Association, for machining apprenticeships).

## Read This Before Signing Anything

Three questions apply to nearly every opportunity here, and the answers determine whether an offer is good:

1. **Am I employed by you, or by a staffing agency?** They look identical in a posting and are different jobs.
2. **Is there a training repayment agreement?** Free training that you must repay if you leave within 18 months is a debt, not a benefit.
3. **Who pays for the certification, and what happens if I fail the exam?** A certification clock without employer support shifts all the risk to you.

Full detail in the [Overview's scam section](OVERVIEW.md#local-scam--caution-notes).
