# Skills & Trades

Paths to learn marketable skills without traditional college.

## What belongs here

- Trade apprenticeships (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, welding, carpentry)
- Coding bootcamps and tech training
- EMT, firefighter, and emergency services training
- Commercial diving certification
- Pilot training and aviation careers
- Maritime careers (merchant marine, yacht crew)
- Heavy equipment operation
- CDL and trucking

## Priority for young adults

Medium-high. Many of these require upfront investment or location commitment, but they build durable skills that enable future freedom.
