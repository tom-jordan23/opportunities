# Service & Volunteer

Structured programs for giving back while gaining experience.

## What belongs here

- Peace Corps
- AmeriCorps (VISTA, NCCC, State programs)
- Workaway and volunteer exchanges
- Teaching English abroad (TEFL/TESOL programs)
- Conservation corps programs
- Disaster relief organizations
- International NGO positions
- National Park Service programs
- Habitat for Humanity

## Priority for Sam

High for programs with stipends or living allowances. Lower priority for purely volunteer positions unless they provide housing and food.
