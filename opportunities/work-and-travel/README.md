# Work & Travel

Opportunities that combine earning money with experiencing new places.

## What belongs here

- Working holiday visas and programs
- Hostel and hospitality work abroad
- Seasonal jobs (ski resorts, harvest work, summer camps)
- Cruise ship positions
- WWOOFing and farm stays
- Au pair and childcare work
- Resort and tourism jobs

## Priority for Sam

High. These opportunities typically provide housing and income—solving the limited funds constraint while enabling travel.
